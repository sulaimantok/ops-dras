## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-30

### Added

- Multi-cluster OpenStack Cinder backup and restore orchestration.
- Persistent PostgreSQL task queue with a dedicated worker.
- Same-cluster and cross-cluster restore flows.
- Encrypted cluster credentials, CSRF protection, RBAC, and audit logging.
- Dashboard, task progress, filters, pagination, and administration UI.
- Alembic migrations, PostgreSQL backups, tests, and CI validation.
